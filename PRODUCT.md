# DS Bot Product Context

## Register

product

Source: [Plugin and host](docs/vision.md#plugin-and-host) and [project overview](AGENTS.md#项目概览): a working team interface hosted by DSH Desktop or Web, not a marketing surface.

## Users

People who chat with one Bot or coordinate a persistent team with clear roles, and need to see who is doing what. Sources: [What DS Bot is](docs/vision.md#what-ds-bot-is), [core requirements](docs/vision.md#ten-core-requirements).

## Product Purpose

Provide a persistent team of independent Bots, with long-term memory, group collaboration, and checkable results using public DSH plugin APIs. Sources: [vision](docs/vision.md#what-ds-bot-is), [requirements](docs/vision.md#ten-core-requirements), [project overview](AGENTS.md#项目概览).

## Brand Personality

Reliable, polished, quiet: act on evidence, provide a polished interface with smooth motion, and remain still without user action or state changes. These descriptions summarize explicit requirements, not a new brand direction. Sources: [requirements 2 and 8](docs/vision.md#ten-core-requirements), [UI principles](docs/design/ui-standard.md#1-设计原则).

## Anti-references

Do not copy other products' interface code, icons or graphics; do not expose development discussions or speculative plans as interface copy. Preserve the existing visual system instead of redesigning it. Sources: [UI usage rules](docs/design/ui-standard.md#0-怎么用这份标准), [copy](docs/design/ui-standard.md#11-文案), [hard rules](AGENTS.md#硬性规则).

## Design Principles

- Correct Bot behavior comes first; results must be verifiable. Source: [trade-offs](docs/vision.md#principles-for-trade-offs).
- Keep team data on the user's computer. Source: [trade-offs](docs/vision.md#principles-for-trade-offs).
- Done means tested, with actual interface inspection for UI changes. Source: [trade-offs](docs/vision.md#principles-for-trade-offs).
- Preserve the established UI, communicate only information users need, and keep source copy English with runtime translations. Sources: [UI usage](docs/design/ui-standard.md#0-怎么用这份标准), [copy](docs/design/ui-standard.md#11-文案).

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain keyboard operation, visual-order tab navigation, Escape dismissal, appropriate ARIA labels and roles, visible focus rings, theme-derived contrast colors, and reduced-motion behavior. The cited standard does not specify a WCAG conformance level; this document does not invent one. Source: [accessibility](docs/design/ui-standard.md#12-可访问性).
